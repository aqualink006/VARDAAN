01 — Origin

The night
she found
her light.

Vardaan was not born a hero. She became one when the darkness around her became impossible to ignore.

On a night when her city lost its power, she discovered a mysterious lantern glowing with a light unlike anything she had ever seen.

The lantern did not give her strength to rule others. It gave her the courage to protect them.
